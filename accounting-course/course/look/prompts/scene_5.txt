SCENE: "The business is its own person." Daylight, split stage. Meera stands on the left holding her own small purse. On the right, the chai stall has become a friendly character itself — the striped awning reads like eyebrows, two round lamp-eyes, the counter edge a smile. A dashed line on the ground separates them. A neat bundle of rupee notes floats in an arc from Meera's purse across the line into the stall's open galla, with a little sparkle trail. Khata watches from the bottom corner, pleased. Clear, readable staging, characters large in frame.
