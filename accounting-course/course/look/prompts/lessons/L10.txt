LESSON 10 · MONTH-END SURPRISES. Evening at the stall. Meera tosses confetti in celebration — but the confetti is frozen mid-air. Khata holds up a hand 'wait'. Three surprises around her: an electricity bill envelope fluttering down, a jar of tea leaves being counted with only a little left, and the chai cart with its 'value' visibly draining as a trickle of coins into an expense jar.
