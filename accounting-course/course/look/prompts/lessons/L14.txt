LESSON 14 · WHERE DID THE MONEY GO? Night at the stall again (rhymes with lesson 1), but happy: Meera smiles holding open Khata, who glows. Above them, a cheerful 'waterfall' of coins pours from labelled sources (Meera's savings purse, Ravi Mama's umbrella, a chai cup) into the galla, with a few coins flowing out to a cart and a home. Khata waves at the viewer, offering itself — 'your turn'.
