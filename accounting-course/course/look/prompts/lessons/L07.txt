LESSON 7 · THE GOLDEN RULES, DECODED. Split scene. Left half: a traditional Indian merchant sitting on a white floor cushion (gaddi) with a red bahi-khata and a brass oil lamp, writing with a pen. Right half: Khata standing next to the balance scale. Between them, three arched doorways with simple icons above each — a person, a box, and a receipt — with glowing lines connecting matching entries on both sides. Warm golden accents.
