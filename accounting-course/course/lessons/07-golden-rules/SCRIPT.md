## Line 5 — Three rules, translated (Scene 5)

**Time:** 1:55 – 2:43
**Delivery:** Each rule said crisply, like a quote; each translation slower and warmer — "you already know this".

    Each family has one rule.
    Real: debit what comes in, credit what goes out. [PAUSE 0.6s]
    The translation? Real accounts are assets. Something coming in grows an asset, and assets grow on the left. Same thing.
    Personal: debit the receiver, credit the giver. [PAUSE 0.6s]
    When someone gives the stall something, like a loan, the stall now owes them.
    A liability grows, on the right. A credit. [PAUSE 0.5s]
    And when someone receives from the stall, they owe us, or we owe them less. That's the left. A debit.
    Nominal: debit all expenses, credit all incomes. [PAUSE 0.6s]
    Expenses shrink equity, so they're debits. Incomes grow it, so they're credits.
