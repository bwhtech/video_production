## Scene 1 — Night at the stall (cold open)

- scene: Night, Apr 2 — her first trading day. Meera alone at her chai stall, galla stuffed with notes. She shrugs.
- duration: 30s
- voiceover: "This is Meera… Did Meera make money today? She doesn't know." (Lines 1–2)
- learning: Create the question the whole course answers: "Did I make money?"
- transition_in: fade from black
- transition_out: galla lid closes → its brass clasp becomes a clay token (Scene 2)
- status: outline

**Visual.** Wide, warm night street. String lights over a small wooden stall with a hand-painted
board "MEERA'S CHAI" (the one allowed in-world sign; dubbing keeps it). Kettle steam curls. Meera
(apron, bun with pencil) sits on an upturned crate, galla on her knees, notes spilling. Empty
glasses stacked. A street dog snoozes. **Calendar strip** (kit `calendarStrip`, bible §5.7) along the top, dim
night tint, `2` highlighted — the night is dated: Apr 2, first trading day (bible §2). Numbers only; the month label
is small and swappable.

**Motion.** Slow push-in (1.00 → **1.06** scale over 15s — house cap). Meera counts notes with a flick-flick
loop, stops, looks at camera, slow head tilt (15 fps steps). On "She doesn't know" — tiny shrug with
one settle on the apron strings (no continuing sway); a "?" emote drop-and-places above her (no words, no
pop-in). On "most people…" a few other stall-owners' silhouettes in neighbouring stalls also shrug in a staggered
wave (gag: everyone's confused). Calendar strip drop-and-places on frame 1 and then sits dead still.

**Sound.** Night ambience (distant traffic, crickets), kettle bubble, paper-note rustle on each
flick. Music: none until Line 2; soft pizzicato pickup enters on "By the end of this course…"

**Transition.** Meera snaps the galla shut (squash on lid, SFX: wooden clack). Camera rushes into
the brass clasp; it fills frame and morphs into a clay token on a sandy background.

## Scene 2 — Accounting is counting

- scene: Clay tokens count sheep → Pacioli's quill writes two columns → bahi-khata → phone app.
- duration: 32s
- voiceover: "Accounting… started with counting… Mesopotamia… Pacioli… bahi-khata… apps." (Line 3)
- learning: Accounting is old, simple at heart (counting), and universal.
- transition_out: phone screen goes dark → reflection shows a sleeping red book (Khata)
- status: outline

**Visual.** One continuous horizontal "time-scroll" — a single long illustrated strip the camera
pans along (no cuts):
1. **~3000 BCE** — sandy ground, a farmer pressing wedge marks into a clay tablet; three
   cartoon sheep hop past and a tally mark appears for each.
2. **1494 Venice** — the strip turns to a canal with a gondola; Pacioli (friendly monk, round
   glasses) writes in a big book; two neat columns ink themselves in.
3. **India** — a merchant's gaddi (floor cushion) with a red bahi-khata, brass lamp; the book
   is tied with string.
4. **Today** — a phone on a café table, an app list scrolling.
Small year chips (`3000 BCE`, `1494`, `today`) — numbers only, no words, dub-proof.

**Motion.** Camera tracks right at a steady pace with a slight ease at each era so each holds
~5s. Each era has one "something happens" action: sheep hop + tally; quill writes; string on the
bahi-khata unties itself; phone buzzes. Parallax: foreground props move 1.3×.

**Sound.** Era-flavoured stingers layered over the music bed: clay tap, quill scratch, tabla
"dha", phone buzz. Music: light pizzicato + marimba bed starts here (≈0.12 under VO, ducked).

**Transition.** The phone screen goes dark; in its black glass, a reflection of a red book
appears; camera pushes through the glass into Scene 3.

## Scene 3 — Meet Khata

- scene: Khata wakes up, stretches, opens itself: left page / right page glow.
- duration: 15s
- voiceover: "Meet Khata… An open book has two sides. A left page, and a right page." (Line 4)
- learning: Plant the left/right idea (pays off in Lesson 6: debit = left, credit = right).
- transition_out: Khata's two pages lift off and fly apart as two cards (Scene 4)
- status: outline

**Visual.** Plain warm background (cream). Khata centre-frame: red cloth cover, cream pages,
gold string, two big expressive eyes on the cover. Small ground shadow.

**Motion.** Khata asleep (slow breathing scale 1.00↔1.02 — this is a *performance* beat, it ends
when it wakes). Eyes blink open on "Meet Khata", stretch (squash-and-stretch hop), opens itself
like a butterfly. On "a left page" the left page brightens + small arrow bob ←; on "a right page"
the right page brightens + arrow →. On "It's going to matter" Khata winks.

**Sound.** Yawn-like soft synth "boing" on the hop, page-flutter on open, two soft "tink" chimes
for left/right (left = lower pitch, right = higher — reuse this pitch pair every time debit/credit
appears later in the course).

**Transition.** The two pages detach and slide outward to become two large cards that hold
Scene 4's two questions.

## Scene 4 — Two questions, two reports

- scene: Two cards: "Making money?" (₹ + up/down arrow icon) and "Own & owe?" (box + hand icons). They become a film strip (P&L) and a polaroid (Balance Sheet).
- duration: 50s
- voiceover: "Two questions… Profit and Loss… a movie… Balance Sheet… a photo." (Line 5)
- learning: The P&L covers a period; the Balance Sheet is one moment.
- transition_out: the polaroid develops into a photo of Meera's stall → camera enters the photo (Scene 5)
- status: outline

**Visual.** Left card: Lucide `trending-up` + `indian-rupee` icons. Right card: `package` + `hand-coins`.
On "a chai stall, a startup, a giant company" three tiny owners (chai-wali, hoodie founder,
suited CEO) each drop-and-place holding the same two cards — universality gag.
Then:
- Left card unrolls into the **film strip** (kit `filmStrip`, bible §5.11 — the SAME component L12 builds the P&L
  in, with its two ruled result frames for Gross / Net visible but empty here): frames show Apr 1… Apr 30 of the
  stall (sales, rent, a coin stream). Label chip: `Profit & Loss`.
- Right card becomes the **two-column polaroid** (kit `polaroid`, §5.11 — the SAME object L13/L14 develop the
  Balance Sheet in; date slot reads `30 Apr`) with a camera-flash: the photo shows the stall with its things (cart,
  galla, stock) on the left column and two small claim TAGS on strings (Meera's face, Ravi Mama's face — icons
  only, no amounts, no words) on the right column. Label chip: `Balance Sheet`.
Labels: report names appear as short chips (the terms we're teaching — they stay).

**Motion.** Cards drop-and-place (stagger 0.15s, no bounce). Owners drop-and-place on beat (no pop-in overshoot).
Film strip scrolls continuously while on screen (it's literally a movie) then stops. Polaroid: shutter flash (white
flash 2 frames at 40%), print slides out and develops (desaturated → colour over 1s). On "A movie, and a photo" both
items nudge toward camera together (one move, power2.out, settle).

**Sound.** Projector clatter loop under the film strip; camera shutter + print whirr on the
polaroid. Small "ding" when each label chip lands.

**Transition.** The polaroid's photo of the stall grows to fill frame and comes alive — we're
back at the stall in daylight (Scene 5).

## Scene 5 — The business is its own person

- scene: Meera and her stall become two characters side by side; inside a dashed "imagine" card, ₹50,000 crosses from Meera to the stall's galla; the galla gets a claim tag — Meera's face + `₹50,000` (no words).
- duration: 65s
- voiceover: "Meera and Meera's Chai are not the same… business entity." (Line 6)
- learning: Business entity concept; capital belongs to the business; personal ≠ business spending.
- misconception: "It's my stall, so its money is my money."
- transition_out: the stall's galla flips open → coins jump out as game tokens (Scene 6)
- status: outline

**Visual.** Daylight. Split stage: Meera stage-left with her own purse/phone; the stall
stage-right. For this lesson only, the stall gets a face (awning = eyebrows, two round lamp-eyes,
counter edge = smile) to sell "its own little person". A dashed line on the ground between them.
- The ₹50,000 drop is HYPOTHETICAL here (the VO says "when Meera takes…"; the real drop is L2 s01, Apr 1 dawn), so
  it plays inside the dashed **"imagine" card** (kit `imagineCard`, dashed cream border — the shared hypothetical
  device, also L3 s09 / L4): the card drop-and-places over the split stage; inside it, ₹50,000 as a neat bundle of
  notes floats from Meera's purse across the line into the galla; a small claim TAG on a string hangs off the galla:
  Meera's face icon + `₹50,000` (no words — not an IOU; §5.2). On "the stall remembers that it came from Meera" the
  tag turns once to camera. The card lifts off before the misconception beat; the tag stays on the galla.
- Misconception: Meera's thought bubble shows galla = her purse merged into one blob; Khata (small, corner) stamps a
  red ✗ on it.
- Two quick examples as mini-vignettes on the split stage:
  (a) Meera reaches into the galla to pay a home electricity bill — a `zap` medallion with a small `map-pin` badge
  (electricity · home side; icon registry §5.12, `house` is retired) → the stall's face frowns, ✗ stamp.
  (b) Meera pays for sugar with her own phone → sugar goes to the stall; stall's face perks up, a second small tag
  hangs off the sugar: Meera's face icon + a `₹` glyph (no amount — none in the data file; no words) — the stall
  still records it.
- End: `Business entity` chip lands on the dashed line, which turns solid.

**Motion.** Meera and the stall do a mirrored "hello" wave to establish them as two characters (15 fps steps).
The note bundle follows an arc path (eased, slight spin) with a coin-sparkle trail. Stamp per bible §5.6: scale
1.25 → 1.0 `power3.out`, `stamp_thunk`, NO screen shake, NO settle wobble; Khata is the stamper. Stall face reacts
with a single squash on frown/smile, then holds.

**Sound.** Whoosh + soft cash-register "ka-ching" when the bundle lands. Stamp: heavy rubber
"thunk". Music drops to near-silence on "Meera and Meera's Chai are not the same" (1s hold),
returns on the next sentence. 

**Transition.** The galla's lid flips open (one squash, no bounce) and three coins lift out on eased arcs — they
become the game tokens of Scene 6.

## Scene 6 — Is it a transaction? (worked → faded → solo)

- scene: Game show: four event cards; a big YES (₹ coin) / NO (empty hand) sorter. Khata judges the first, Meera the second, the viewer the last two with countdowns.
- duration: 75s
- voiceover: "Only transactions… Is it a transaction?" (Line 7)
- learning: A transaction changes what the business has or owes and is measurable in money.
- pause_beats: 2 × 1.5s (worked/faded), 2 × 3s countdown (solo)
- transition_out: the sorted YES cards stack into a notebook → the notebook is Khata (Scene 7)
- status: outline

**Visual.** Bright game-show set: two bins — left bin with a ₹ coin icon (transaction), right
bin with an empty open hand (not). Khata sits on a small judge's stool with a tiny bell.
Event cards are pictures, not text:
1. Chai cup + ₹20 coin → **YES** (Khata rings the bell) — *worked*.
2. Meera smiling at customer, heart emote → **NO** (Meera hesitates, tilts to YES, Khata shakes
   head, she flips it to NO) — *faded*: Meera's mistake mirrors the viewer's.
3. Sugar bag + coin → 3-2-1 countdown ring → **YES** — *solo*.
4. Phone ringing with milk-can icon → 3-2-1 → **NO**. On the VO's "Yet." Khata does a GESTURE only — raises one
   page-corner like a finger and winks (no on-screen text, no "…yet" chip; bible §9 on-screen text rule).

**Motion.** Cards flip in from the top (rotateX fake) and drop-and-place into the bins (1.07× lifted → settle,
power2.out, no bounce). Countdown ring drains clockwise over 3s with tick SFX; the answer card lights (flat colour
step, no glow). Khata's bell ring = one small hop (acting beat, lands and holds).

**Sound.** Game-show tick loop during pauses, "ding-ding" for YES, soft "bwomp" (friendly, not
harsh) for NO. Music sits lower during countdowns so the ticks own the pause.

**Transition.** The YES cards fly together and stack into a little notebook; it puffs up and
becomes Khata, who opens — Scene 7.

- transition_out: camera pulls out — everything shrinks into three recap tiles (Scene 8)

**Visual.** Three-step pipeline left → right: (1) slips land on Khata's pages, (2) slips drop into

**Motion.** One continuous left-to-right flow; each step lights when VO names it (flat colour step). On "her first

**Sound.** Paper flick per slip, glass clink per jar, projector click. Three-note rising motif on
"Record. Sort. Summarise." (reuse as the course's "accounting cycle" motif).

**Transition.** Camera dolly-out; the three moments shrink into three recap tiles.
