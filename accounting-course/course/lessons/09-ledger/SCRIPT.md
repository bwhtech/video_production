## Line 3 — One book per account — the ledger (Scene 3)

**Time:** 1:07 – 1:43
**Delivery:** A small reveal. Curious on "Watch.", a quick wink on the Khata callback (the name was explained in L7 — this is a reminder, not a reveal).

    Here's the trick. The journal sorts money by date. But Meera's question is about one account — Cash. So let's sort a second way: by account.
    Remember the account jars? Watch. Each jar becomes a little book. Cash gets one. Bank gets one. Sales, Rent, Ravi Mama's loan — every account gets its own page, with a debit side on the left and a credit side on the right. This set of books is called the **ledger**. And the list of all your accounts, grouped by type, is called the **chart of accounts**. Remember why our friend is called Khata? Khata means account.

## Line 4 — Posting — journal lines fly to their pages (Scene 4)

**Time:** 1:43 – 2:26
**Delivery:** Precise and calm for the rule; rhythmic, almost musical on "Left… right."

    Copying each journal line onto its account's page is called **posting**. And the rule is easy. A debit in the journal goes on the left side of that account. A credit goes on the right.
    Watch the very first entry. Cash, debit, fifty thousand — onto the left side of Cash. Capital, credit, fifty thousand — onto the right side of Capital. Beside each amount, Khata writes the date and the other account, so you can always trace where it came from. Then Khata ticks the journal line, so nothing gets posted twice.
    [SFX: tink_low on each left landing · tink_high on each right landing]
    Now the rest. Seventeen entries, one line at a time. Left… right. Left… right.
