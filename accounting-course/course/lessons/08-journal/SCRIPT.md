    [PAUSE 2.0s]
    [SFX: tick-tock … ding ×3 — a third slot appears]
    Three of them! The loan went down — debit Loan from Ravi Mama, three thousand. Interest is an expense — debit Interest, three hundred. And cash went out — To Cash, three thousand three hundred. Two debits, one credit. That's called a **compound entry** — one line for every account. And the two sides still match — three thousand three hundred each.
    [ON SCREEN: Loan from Ravi Mama A/c … Dr ₹3,000 · Interest A/c … Dr ₹300 · To Cash A/c ₹3,300 · ₹3,300 = ₹3,300]

    [SFX: stamp]
    Nope. Only the three hundred rupees of interest is an expense. The three thousand just gives back money that was never Meera's. It shrinks her debt — not her profit.
